Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

PhotoTrashIT does not collect your data. Your photos are processed on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No developer server. The developer does not receive your photos, their contents, metadata, or your decisions about them.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Photo library access

PhotoTrashIT uses Apple's Photos framework (PhotoKit) to show your photos and, when you confirm, to delete the ones you selected. iOS lets you choose the level of access:

  • Full access — the app can show photos from your whole library.
  • Limited access — the app sees only the photos you selected. You can change the selection at any time.
  • No access — the app cannot show or delete any photos.

You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoTrashIT.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue later, the app saves a small progress file in its own storage on your iPhone. It contains only Apple's local photo identifiers and your decisions (keep or queued for deletion). It does not contain copies of your photos or of your library.

You can clear this data with Reset All Progress in the app (this does not delete any photos), or by deleting the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups under Apple's terms.

Network and Apple services

The app does not send your data anywhere. However, if you use iCloud Photos with optimized storage, iOS may download full-size photos from Apple's iCloud when the app displays them, and deletions sync through iCloud to your other devices. iCloud Photos, iCloud Backup and other Apple system services are provided by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy, not by the developer.

Deleting photos

The app deletes photos only after you review the queue and confirm in the iOS system prompt. Deleted items move to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where they usually remain for about 30 days unless you remove them yourself. PhotoTrashIT never permanently deletes photos and cannot recover photos once they have been permanently deleted. A Live Photo is deleted as a whole, including its motion part.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party trackers, and loads no external scripts or fonts. GitHub may process standard technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Кратко по-русски

PhotoTrashIT не собирает данные: нет аккаунтов, рекламы, аналитики и сервера разработчика. Фото обрабатываются на iPhone, разработчик их не получает. Доступ к фото (полный, выбранные фото или нет доступа) меняется в «Настройки → Конфиденциальность и безопасность → Фото». На устройстве хранится только небольшой файл прогресса с локальными идентификаторами фото и вашими решениями, без копий фото; его удаляет «Reset All Progress» или удаление приложения. При iCloud Фото iOS может загружать снимки из iCloud, а удаление синхронизируется на все устройства. Удалённое попадает в «Недавно удалённые» (обычно около 30 дней или до ручной очистки); приложение не удаляет фото окончательно и не может восстановить окончательно удалённое. Сайт размещён на GitHub Pages без cookies и трекеров; у GitHub есть стандартные серверные логи.

Getting started

  1. Open PhotoTrashIT and allow access to your photos when iOS asks.
  2. Swipe left to add a photo to the deletion queue, or right to keep it. The on-screen buttons do the same. Use Undo to revert your last decision.
  3. Open the queue, remove any photo you want to keep, then choose to delete.
  4. Confirm in the iOS prompt. Only then are photos deleted.

Photo access

  • Full access shows your whole library.
  • Limited access shows only photos you selected. Use Select Photos… in the app to change the selection.
  • No access: the app cannot show photos. Turn access on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoTrashIT.

Deleting photos

  • Swiping never deletes anything. It only adds the photo to the queue.
  • Deleted photos go to Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted. They usually stay there for about 30 days, or until you delete them from that album yourself. To recover a photo, open Recently Deleted and choose Recover.
  • PhotoTrashIT never permanently deletes photos. Once a photo has been permanently deleted (from Recently Deleted or after it expires), the app cannot recover it.
  • With iCloud Photos on, a deletion applies to all your devices that use the same iCloud library.
  • A Live Photo is deleted as a whole, including its motion part.
  • Storage is not freed immediately: deleted items still take space while they are in Recently Deleted, and iCloud may take time to update.

Progress

Your decisions are saved on your iPhone so you can continue later. Reset All Progress clears all decisions and the queue; it does not delete any photos. Deleting the app also removes saved progress.

Photos load slowly

If you use iCloud Photos with Optimize iPhone Storage, iOS may need to download a photo from iCloud before it can be shown. Check your internet connection and try again.

Кратко по-русски

Свайп влево добавляет фото в очередь на удаление, вправо оставляет; сам свайп ничего не удаляет. Удаление происходит только после просмотра очереди и подтверждения в системном окне iOS. Удалённое попадает в «Недавно удалённые» (обычно около 30 дней или до ручной очистки), откуда его можно восстановить. Приложение не удаляет окончательно и не может вернуть окончательно удалённое. С iCloud Фото удаление применяется на всех устройствах; Live Photo удаляется целиком; место освобождается не сразу. Доступ к фото меняется в «Настройки → Конфиденциальность и безопасность → Фото».
